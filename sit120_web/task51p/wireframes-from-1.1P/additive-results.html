Side-by-side additive cards

E1442

Thickener

Hydroxypropyl distarch phosphate is a modified starch. In yoghurt it holds water so the product stays smooth after sitting in a fridge.

Some shoppers prefer pots that rely on milk protein and live cultures instead of added starch.

E440

Gelling agent

Pectin is taken from fruit. It is widely used in jams and spoonable dairy foods to give a soft set.

It is generally treated as a familiar plant fibre rather than a synthetic preservative.

Live cultures

Not an additive code

Streptococcus thermophilus and Lactobacillus bulgaricus turn milk into yoghurt. They appear on the label as ingredients, not E-numbers.

LabelLens lists them so the scan is complete, then marks them as cultures rather than processing additives.
